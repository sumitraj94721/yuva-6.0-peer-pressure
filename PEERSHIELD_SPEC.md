# PeerShield Specification

PeerShield is a privacy-first campus prevention prototype for recognizing peer pressure, practicing refusal, choosing healthy alternatives, finding support, and improving campus culture.

## Scope & Capabilities
1. **Student Intervention Loop**:
   - Private Quick Check-In with local non-diagnostic reflection.
   - Peer Pressure Simulator with scoring, boundary coaching, and confidence tracking.
   - Calm Zone with 60-second breathing reset and healthy alternatives.
   - Progress dashboard tracking student activities anonymously on-device.
   - Resources and emergency guidance directing immediate crises to 112 in India.
   - Anonymous reporting mechanism with unique reference codes.
2. **Personal AI Companion & Live Voice**:
   - PeerShield AI conversational prevention companion at `/ai-companion`.
   - Real-time Gemini Live Voice Session with audio streaming, animated AI Orb, 16-bar Voice Visualizer, and in-memory live transcript.
   - Practice Mode for safe refusal roleplaying with constructive feedback and suggested lines.
   - Multi-language natural dialogue in English, Hindi, and Hinglish.
   - Resilient Demo AI fallback ensuring competition and offline reliability.
3. **Campus Intelligence**:
   - Campus Pulse with clearly labeled illustrative demo data separated from public evidence.
   - Admin Prevention Dashboard and CSV data validation tool for campus surveys.
   - Community Peer Champions showcasing healthy peer norms.

## Safety & Ethics Principles
- **Non-Diagnostic**: PeerShield AI is a supportive companion, not a doctor, therapist, or diagnostic system.
- **Strict Substance Guardrails**: No tips or instructions on acquiring, concealing, or using illegal drugs or alcohol. All queries are redirected toward safety, boundaries, and healthy coping.
- **Crisis Escalation**: Clear guidance to call 112 for immediate emergencies. PeerShield never claims to dispatch emergency responders.
- **Privacy by Default**: No account required, no student profiling, no recording or permanent storage of raw chat transcripts, and sensitive personal data (passwords, addresses) is explicitly discouraged.
