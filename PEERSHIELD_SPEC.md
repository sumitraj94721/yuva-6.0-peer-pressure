# PeerShield Specification

PeerShield is a privacy-first campus prevention prototype for recognizing peer pressure, practicing refusal, choosing healthy alternatives, finding support, and improving campus culture.

## Current scope
Phase 1 foundation is complete. The next implementation batch focuses on the student intervention loop: Check-In, Pressure Simulator, Calm Zone, Progress, Resources, SOS, and Anonymous Report.

## Safety and data principles
- Anonymous by default; prototype data stays in localStorage.
- No diagnosis, addiction score, public student profiles, or substance-use instructions.
- Demo and public evidence data must be labeled and kept separate from campus survey data.
- Emergency guidance points to 112 and does not claim PeerShield provides emergency response.
